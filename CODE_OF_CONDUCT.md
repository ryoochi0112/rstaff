# Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and inclusive environment.

## Our Pledge
We adopt the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, by reference. The full text is available at:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

## Expected and Unacceptable Behaviour
A summary of our expectations and unacceptable actions under this covenant includes:
- **Expected:** Participate in an active, respectful, and collaborative manner.
- **Expected:** Use welcoming, supportive, and inclusive language.
- **Unacceptable:** Harassment, exclusion, public or private intimidation, or personal attacks of any kind.
- **Unacceptable:** Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- **Unacceptable:** Any form of sexualized language or attention.

## Enforcement / Reporting
We take all reports of unacceptable behavior seriously. Report privately through:
- A private GitHub security advisory on this repository: https://github.com/ryoochi0112/rstaff/security/advisories/new
- Or contact the maintainer @ryoochi0112 on GitHub.
